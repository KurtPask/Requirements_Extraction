RMKS/1.  This NAVADMIN announces a modification to the Nuclear Officer Bonus 
and Incentive Pay (NOBIP) Nuclear Officer Accession Bonus (NOAB) eligibility 
of reference (a). 
 
2.  Navy leadership remains committed to recruiting top-performing nuclear-
trained Sailors for the nuclear-trained Limited Duty Officer program.  NOAB 
is a monetary incentive that focuses on improving these recruitment efforts. 
 
3.  The following changes were effective July 24, 2025, for nuclear- trained 
Limited Duty Officers (LDO(N)): 
     a.  NOAB eligibility will be extended to those selected for 
commissioning as an LDO(N) starting in Fiscal Year (FY) 2027. 
     b.  Prospective officers must complete and pass technical screening by 
Naval Reactors to determine suitability for qualification and continued duty 
in connection with the supervision, operation and maintenance of naval 
nuclear propulsion plants. 
     c.  The NOAB rate is $30K for selectees that complete their technical 
interviews and are screened to commission as a nuclear trained officer. 
     d.  LDO(N) NOAB will first be available for Sailors selected at the FY27 
Nuclear Limited Duty Officer In-Service Procurement Board conducted in 
February 2026. 
     e.  Request for payment will be submitted to Navy Personnel Command, 
Submarine and Nuclear Officer Distribution (PERS-42) utilizing enclosure (3) 
of reference (a) as an example.  Payment will be made on the date of 
commission once PERS-42 has accepted the request. 
 
4.  The extended eligibility for NOAB in paragraph 2 modifies the NOAB 
eligibility of reference (a). 
 
5.  All other bonus and incentive pays authorized by reference (a) remain 
unchanged. 
 
6.  For questions regarding nuclear officer bonus incentive pay, nuclear-
trained LDOs should contact PERS-422S at (901) 874-3936/DSN 
884 or via e-mail at mosha.d.williams.civ@us.navy.mil.
